Refund Policy

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Policy explains cancellation and refund handling for paid CycleX Network software subscriptions processed through Paddle.

1. Scope

This Refund Policy applies to paid CycleX Network software subscriptions purchased through Paddle. It does not apply to blockchain network fees, on-chain transactions, CYCX tokens or other digital assets that are not sold through the Paddle subscription checkout.

Paddle is Merchant of Record. Paddle processes payments, taxes, receipts, cancellations and refunds for Paddle-processed CycleX Network subscriptions. Mandatory consumer rights always apply where required by law.

2. Standard refund position

Except where required by law, or where a refund is approved under this Policy or Paddle’s applicable buyer/refund terms, paid subscription charges are generally non-refundable after access to the paid service has been provided. We do not normally provide prorated refunds for unused time after a subscription is canceled.

3. When a refund may be appropriate

You may contact us or Paddle if you believe a refund should be considered, including for:

  • a duplicate or incorrect charge;
  • a material technical defect that prevents access to the paid service and cannot be resolved within a reasonable time;
  • an unauthorized transaction, subject to verification;
  • a statutory withdrawal or refund right that applies in your jurisdiction;
  • another case where Paddle determines a refund is appropriate under its policies.

4. Statutory rights

Nothing in this Policy limits rights that cannot be waived under applicable consumer law. Paddle’s current refund policy includes country-specific withdrawal rights and may provide rights that are broader than this standard CycleX Network policy. Where mandatory law or Paddle’s buyer terms provide greater protection, those rules apply.

5. How to request a refund

For a Paddle-processed purchase, you can request billing or refund assistance through Paddle.net or the support/manage-subscription link in your Paddle receipt or Customer Portal. You may also contact support@cyclex.network so we can help investigate a product or access issue. Any approved refund for a Paddle-processed transaction is issued through Paddle, not by sending funds directly from CycleX Network.

6. Subscription cancellation

You may cancel an active subscription through the Paddle Customer Portal or the subscription-management controls made available by CycleX Network. Unless mandatory law or Paddle’s terms provide otherwise, cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. You retain paid access through that period and will not be charged for the next renewal.

7. Abuse and fraud

Refunds may be declined where there is evidence of fraud, refund abuse, chargeback abuse or other manipulative behavior, subject to applicable law and Paddle’s policies.

8. Contact

For product or billing questions, contact support@cyclex.network.
