Privacy Notice

Effective date: September 28, 2026

This Notice explains what information CycleX Network processes, why we process it, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This Privacy Notice explains how CycleX Network handles personal information when you visit our websites, create an account, use the dashboard, APIs, monitoring and security tools, contact us, or purchase a software subscription.

2. Information we collect

Account and contact information

We may receive your name, email address, account identifiers and authentication information from our identity provider when you sign in, as well as information you provide when contacting support or sales.

Billing information

Paid subscription payments are processed by Paddle as Merchant of Record. We may receive billing-related identifiers, subscription status, plan, billing cycle, transaction metadata and customer identifiers needed to provide access and support. CycleX Network does not need to store your full payment-card number for Paddle-processed transactions.

Service and technical usage

We may collect IP address, browser or device information, timestamps, request metadata, API usage, rate-limit information, error logs, security logs, webhook delivery metadata and product interactions needed to operate, secure and improve the Services.

Blockchain and monitoring inputs

If you submit wallet addresses, token addresses, contract addresses, transaction hashes or other blockchain identifiers, we process them to provide the requested security, monitoring or intelligence function. Blockchain information may already be public and immutable.

Analytics and measurement

Our websites may use analytics and conversion-measurement technologies, including Google Analytics and X measurement tools, as well as essential technologies from service providers used for hosting, authentication and payments. These technologies may process device, browser, page-view and interaction data.

3. How we use information

  • provide, authenticate and operate the Services;
  • enforce plan entitlements, usage limits and security controls;
  • process billing state and provide subscription support;
  • detect abuse, fraud, attacks and operational problems;
  • respond to support, sales and legal requests;
  • analyze and improve reliability, usability and product performance;
  • comply with legal obligations and protect our rights and users.

4. Service providers

We use service providers to run parts of the Services. Depending on the feature, these may include Cloudflare for hosting and network/security infrastructure, Clerk for authentication, Paddle for payments and subscription commerce, and analytics or measurement providers such as Google and X. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices and, where applicable, on our behalf.

5. Legal bases and required disclosures

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as necessary to perform our contract with you, pursue legitimate interests in operating and securing the Services, comply with legal obligations, and obtain consent where consent is legally required. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights or safety, or in connection with a corporate transaction subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Retention

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including account operation, security, audit, billing, dispute resolution and legal compliance. Public blockchain data may remain permanently available on the underlying network even if we stop associating it with your account.

7. Security

We use technical and organizational measures designed to protect information, including access controls and encrypted secrets where appropriate. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security, so you should also protect your account credentials and API keys.

8. International processing

CycleX Network and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use or rely on recognized legal mechanisms for international transfers.

9. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. You may also control certain cookies or tracking technologies through browser settings or any consent controls that are made available.

10. Children

The Services are not directed to children who are not legally able to enter into the relevant agreement in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children for paid subscriptions.

11. Changes to this Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice as the Services, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above shows when this version became effective.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or rights requests may be sent to support@cyclex.network.
